How BloodLink works

Three steps from urgent need to a confirmed helper.
Step 1

Post the need

Share the patient's blood group, hospital, city and how soon it is needed. Your phone number stays tucked away.

Step 2

Get matched

We rank compatible, available and nearby donors. Donors can offer to help directly on the request.

Step 3

Connect safely

Accept one offer to reveal contact details to each other. After the donation, record it and start the cooldown.
